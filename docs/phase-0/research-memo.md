# Phase 0 research memo — access-gap

This memo is generated by `research/phase0/render_docs.py` from harness
`results.json` files. Narrative is the template; every quantitative claim is
substituted from those files. Re-run `make research` after changing a harness.

**Safety.** This project is analytical research. It is not a care navigator,
not a coverage determination, and not medical advice. Numbers in Phase 0
are a committed 3-state stand-in, not a live CMS or Census pull.

---

## §1 Problem statement

Approved gene therapies exist. The people who can actually reach them are
a county-sized subset of the people who are theoretically eligible.
Distance to a certified center, Medicaid coverage of that specific
product, insurance completeness, and the width of the published
prevalence range are four different maps. Collapsing them into a single
undocumented score hides who is left out.

The people who pay for the hide are state Medicaid agencies that think
"covered" means "reachable," manufacturers that site centers on
coastlines, and families who discover the travel is eight visits, not
one. The obvious fix — a national average travel time — fails when a
county is 14 miles from a center that the state's Medicaid program does
not pay for, or 360 miles from one that it does.

This repo treats county × therapy as the analytical unit and measures
whether a composite access index is even a stable object.

## §2 Prior art

| Tool | What it does | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KFF / NCSL Medicaid trackers | State-level coverage bits | Not county; not therapy-specific travel |
| Manufacturer certified-center lists | Addresses | No multi-visit burden; no denominator |
| ICER gene-therapy reviews | Value and budget impact | Not geography |
| Orphanet / CDC prevalence | Ranges, often wide | Usually collapsed to a midpoint in dashboards |
| HRSA shortage areas / RUCA | Rurality | Not therapy-specific |
| County Health Rankings | Composite county scores | Not gene therapy; weights are opaque |

Something already "solves" access maps: several commercial site-of-care
tools exist. Another warehouse is still worth building because (1) the
unit is county × therapy, not state or patient, (2) travel is multi-visit
rather than a one-way mile, (3) prevalence stays an interval, and (4)
index weights are explicit enough to fail a rank-correlation test.

## §4 Technical selection

Each choice below is expensive to reverse. Options, criterion, measurement,
decision.

### 4.1 Which sources are usable

Options: use every table; drop incomplete sources; impute.

Criterion: completeness ≥ 0.80 and freshness ≤ 365 days against the
pinned evaluation date. Missing ACS is not imputed to zero.

Measurement: `research/phase0/source_coverage/` (DuckDB staging).

| source | retrieved | freshness days | n | n missing | completeness | decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| cms_medicaid | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 9 | 0 | 1.000 | usable |
| acs_s2701 | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 12 | 1 | 0.917 | usable with explicit missing handling (never impute zero) |
| travel_standin | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 36 | 0 | 1.000 | usable |
| orphanet_cdc | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 4 | 0 | 1.000 | usable |
| capacity_sparse | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 2 | 1 | 0.500 | not usable as a primary index input |
| gazetteer | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 12 | 0 | 1.000 | usable |
| center_list | 2026-01-15 | 264 | 3 | 0 | 1.000 | usable |

Decision: **Usable: cms_medicaid, acs_s2701, travel_standin, orphanet_cdc, gazetteer, center_list. Not usable as a primary index input: capacity_sparse. ACS missingness on Gilmer is kept as missing.**

### 4.2 Composite index weights

Options: a single unpublished weight vector; equal weights; geography
only; coverage only; travel-heavy; drop the composite.

Criterion: minimum pairwise Spearman ρ across schemes on complete cases.
If ρ < 0.70, the index needs rethinking.

Measurement: `research/phase0/index_sensitivity/` (n=33 complete pairs).

| scheme A | scheme B | n | Spearman |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| default | equal | 33 | 0.998 |
| default | geography_only | 33 | 0.846 |
| default | coverage_only | 33 | 0.893 |
| default | travel_heavy | 33 | 0.926 |
| equal | geography_only | 33 | 0.835 |
| equal | coverage_only | 33 | 0.905 |
| equal | travel_heavy | 33 | 0.919 |
| geography_only | coverage_only | 33 | 0.632 |
| geography_only | travel_heavy | 33 | 0.955 |
| coverage_only | travel_heavy | 33 | 0.757 |

Min Spearman = 0.632.

Decision: **The composite access index needs rethinking; rankings are not stable across defensible weight schemes (min Spearman 0.632 < 0.70).**

Weights live in `src/access_gap/weights.py` and are configurable. Travel
burden is `one_way_miles * 2 * visit_multiplier`, not straight-line only.

### 4.3 Prevalence representation

Options: midpoint; low end; high end; interval.

Criterion: if any published range is ≥ 10×, a silent point estimate is a
bug.

Measurement: `research/phase0/prevalence_bounds/`. McDowell × Luxturna
ratio = 10.0. Wide pairs = 1.

| role | fips | therapy | prev low /100k | prev high /100k | ratio | eligible low | eligible high | form |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| best_case | 06075 | zolgensma | 8 | 10 | 1.250 | 64.64 | 80.80 | narrower interval |
| distance_dominated | 48105 | zolgensma | 8 | 10 | 1.250 | 0.25 | 0.31 | narrower interval |
| coverage_gap | 48201 | casgevy | 25 | 40 | 1.600 | 1200.00 | 1920.00 | narrower interval |
| wide_prevalence | 54047 | luxturna | 0.1 | 1 | 10.000 | 0.02 | 0.18 | interval |
| missing_acs | 54021 | zolgensma | 8 | 10 | 1.250 | 0.59 | 0.74 | narrower interval |

Decision: **1 county×therapy pair(s) have a published prevalence range ≥ 10× (54047×luxturna). Eligible population is an interval. Phase 2 must not pick a silent point estimate.**

### 4.4 Warehouse

Options: a spreadsheet; dbt on a warehouse; DuckDB staging → intermediate
→ marts.

Criterion: local, queryable, no service. Completeness queries must run
in `make research`.

Decision: **DuckDB with explicit schemas `staging`, `intermediate`,
`marts`.** A hosted warehouse is Phase 3 if a national extract needs it.
DuckDB vs BigQuery scan time on a national file is **unmeasured**.

## §5 Evaluation design

Hold-out (Phase 3, not run here): a dated national county × therapy file
with a pinned manifest. Until then, numbers are the 3-state slice.

Metrics, declared before any scoring API is built:

- Source completeness and freshness (this phase)
- Spearman rank correlation across documented weight schemes
- Prevalence interval width and eligible-population interval
- Share of counties with missing ACS handled as missing
- Travel-burden ratio: multi-visit effective miles / one-way miles
- Later: agreement with a hand-checked 20-county audit set
- Later: correlation of the index with observed utilization (unmeasured)

Split: the 3-state slice is the entire Phase 0 universe. Seed is not
used; rows are committed.

**Failure condition (written before the answer):** the project fails if a
later national index is published from a single unpublished weight
vector after this memo recorded rank instability, **or** if eligible
population is stored as a midpoint, **or** if missing ACS is imputed to
zero, **or** if travel is one-way miles only.

National utilization correlation is **unmeasured**. The measurement that
would settle it is a CMS claim extract joined to the same county ×
therapy grain.

## §6 Cost and scale model

Phase 0 is local CPU: 36 county × therapy rows and three deterministic
harnesses. Numbers:

- DuckDB in-memory staging of the committed CSVs
- Live CMS scrape wall-clock: **unmeasured**
- Mapbox / OSRM driving-time vs haversine error: **unmeasured**
- Center slot-capacity collection cost: **unmeasured**
- National 3,000-county Spearman: **unmeasured** (only this slice)

Unmeasured: family out-of-pocket, prior-auth denial rates, lodging
nights. This project will not claim those in Phase 0–3 without a source.

## §7 Risks

| Risk | Type | Mitigation or acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Designed miles ≠ driving time | Data | Accepted. Memo says stand-in. OSRM refresh is the retirement test. |
| "Covered" hides prior auth | Data | Keep the bit; do not recode as reachable. |
| Missing ACS imputed to 0 | Technical | Gilmer. A zero here is a failing test. |
| 10× prevalence collapsed to a mean | Technical | Interval type has no default midpoint. |
| Index treated as a patient score | Legal | Disclaimer. Unit is county × therapy. |
| Composite published after rank swing | Scope | Memo decision is binding until a new harness says otherwise. |

### Highest-risk technical unknowns going into Phase 1

1. Whether a national file reproduces the low geography-vs-coverage
   Spearman measured here (national ρ **unmeasured**).

   once the multi-visit multiplier is applied.
3. Whether capacity data can ever clear the 0.80 completeness bar.
